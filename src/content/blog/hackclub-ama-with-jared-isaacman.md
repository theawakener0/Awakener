I don’t know what to say. It's like a dream come true, but let's focus on the main point and try to digest what Jared Isaacman said in this AMA.

When Jared talked about himself and mentioned that he dropped out of high school to build a company, this statement shocked me, and I wondered how a high school dropout could be the Head of NASA, but later in the AMA, he said that you must be passionate about what you do, and don’t be afraid to make mistakes because by making mistakes you will learn, you must be curious. All of that made me reason this decision of dropping out, it says that if you are really passionate and curious about something, you must act, you must not be afraid.

Someone asked Jared what he would do if he were 15 now. He said he would study, and that's because, currently, in my position as the head of NASA, I see many things, and I must be so prepared and educated about the new things happening. And that really shows that we must keep learning and studying for the future.

Another question was asked, which was about how I can become what I want in the future, and what positions I should focus on. Jared answered and said that the positions in the future will be 10x the positions now, so you only need to focus deeply on what you want, and apply to internships to help reach what you want. From this answer, I think we should learn more, keep trying new things, and try to apply to things that will boost our chances of getting what we want.

The AMA was so technical and fun. Jared mentioned how they will put an interplanetary spacecraft powered with nuclear energy (Space Reactor-1 (SR-1) Freedom), and how that will affect the change of missions in space. He also talked about going back to the moon and how the missions like Artemis II will be like a yearly thing. He said that if we want to go to Mars, we must first test and try everything on the moon.

At the end of the AMA, Zack, the co-founder of HackClub, asked Jared what you would do if you were sent back to 500 B.C as the dictator of Earth, and you know that in 2026 A.D, a war would happen between 5 Earths. What are you going to do as the dictator to ensure that your Earth will win the war? Jared answered and said unity, I will try to make the Earth united, hunger for knowledge, kindness, creativity, and not be stopped by ownership or the environment that you are around. Jared's answer was so meaningful, especially the last part, that you must not be stopped by anything, and just keep going, keep yourself hungrier for knowledge, and passionate about what you are doing.

It was really a great AMA, and Jared ended by saying that he is so happy to see people building and learning, and that shows that they are above the curve, and these are the people we need in NASA.
